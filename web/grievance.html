What you can raise here

  • Any other complaint regarding Pappal's services

How to file a grievance

Send an email to the Grievance Officer address above with the subject line "Grievance — [brief topic]". Your email should include:

  • Your name and the email address you use on Pappal
  • A clear description of your complaint
  • The date the issue occurred
  • Any relevant transaction IDs, message IDs, or screenshots

You may also write by post to the registered address above, marking the envelope "Grievance — Pappal".

Response timelines

Acknowledgement

We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours of receipt on business days. If you file outside business hours, the clock starts on the next business day.

Resolution

We aim to resolve most grievances within 15 days of acknowledgement. Complex matters that require investigation may take up to 30 days; we will notify you if that is the case.

Escalation

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate to the relevant regulatory authority. For digital intermediary matters in India, this is the Appellate Committee established under the IT Rules, 2021.

Data-related requests

Requests to access, correct, or delete your personal data can be sent to the Grievance Officer or to our general support address. See the privacy policy and delete your data for more detail.
