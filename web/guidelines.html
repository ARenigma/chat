Community guidelines

Pappal is a place for kind, one-to-one conversations. These guidelines are for everyone, members and listeners alike. They are a friendly summary of our terms, so we can all feel comfortable here.

Be kind

Talk to the other person the way you would like to be spoken to. It is fine to disagree, just do it gently. If a conversation does not feel right for you, you are always free to stop replying or to block the person.

Let the conversation stay on Pappal

Please do not share or ask for phone numbers, email addresses, social media names, links, UPI IDs or bank details. This keeps everyone safe, and it applies to profiles and public posts too. If someone asks you to move the chat elsewhere, you can simply say no.

Things we ask everyone to steer clear of

  • Sexual content, solicitation, grooming or exploitation.
  • Bullying, harassment, threats, hate speech or getting around a block.
  • Anything that sexualises, exploits or puts a minor at risk.
  • Anything illegal.

Be yourself

Please do not pretend to be someone else. Listeners are here to listen and support, so they should not present themselves as a licensed doctor, lawyer, financial adviser or other regulated professional, or offer advice in place of qualified help. Astrologers' readings are for guidance and entertainment, and we ask that they are shared that way.

Keep payments inside the app

Please do not ask for money or gifts, or arrange any payment, outside Pappal. If something looks wrong with a payment, our refund policy explains what we can do, and you can always write to us at hello@pappal.live.

Respect each other's privacy

Members appear on Pappal under pseudonyms. Please do not try to find out who someone really is, or to reach them anywhere except on Pappal. What was said in a conversation, and the other person's personal details, are best kept between the two of you.

If you would like to block someone

Open the chat, tap the menu at the top and choose Block. It happens straight away, and the other person is not told. Neither of you can message the other after that. You can change your mind any time in Settings, under Blocked people.

If something does not feel right

Open the chat, tap the menu at the top and choose Report. To report a particular message, press and hold it and tap Report. Pick the reason that fits best, and add a few words if you like. You can send the report on its own, or choose Submit and Block to do both together.

A real person on our team reads every report. A copy of the message is attached, so there is no need to send screenshots.

How we look after the community

If someone is not following these guidelines, we may pause their account. We look at the conversation, and for a report that means the message attached to it. Reports are kept for 90 days with the reporter's identity removed, as explained on the privacy page.

If you are going through a hard time

Please reach out to local emergency services or a helpline near you. Pappal is not an emergency service, and we want you to get help quickly.
