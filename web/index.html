How it works

Three clear steps from sign-in to conversation.

1

Sign in

Continue with Google and choose the display name shown in the app. Your email is not shown to other members.

2

Discover people

Explore profiles by language, interests, and availability to find a conversation that feels relevant.

3

Start a conversation

Connect by text, voice, or video. Leave whenever you want, and use block or report whenever something feels wrong.

Safety by default

Designed for respectful interaction

  • Private conversations

    Conversations are readable only by the two people in them — enforced in the database, not just in the app.

  • No data sold

    No advertising ID, no contacts, no location. We count events like "a message was sent", never their contents.

  • Block and report

    Block anyone instantly — they are not told. Reports go to a person who reads them, not an automated queue.

  • Reviewed community

    Listener applications are reviewed before profiles appear. Clear community standards prohibit age-inappropriate content, bullying, harassment, and requests to move conversations off-platform.

Pappal is a social conversation platform. It does not provide medical advice, counselling, or emergency services.
