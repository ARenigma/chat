Simple by design

How it works

Three steps, no forms, no waiting room.

1

Sign in

One tap with Google. Your name and email stay private — you pick a display name the app shows instead.

2

Browse and pick someone

See who is available right now — their languages, a short intro, and how quickly they usually reply.

3

Chat or call

Start a text chat or audio call. You are charged per minute while you talk. A message that goes unanswered costs nothing.

Your safety, by default

Private and secure

  • Only the two of you

    Conversations are readable only by the two people in them — enforced in the database, not just in the app.

  • No data sold

    No advertising ID, no contacts, no location. We count events like "a message was sent", never their contents.

  • Block and report

    Block anyone instantly — they are not told. Reports go to a person who reads them, not an automated queue.

  • Adults only

    Everyone who listens is verified 18 or over. Applications are reviewed by a person, not a bot.

Pappal is peer support, not therapy or counselling. The people who listen are not licensed clinicians.

1 token = ₹1. Chat and audio calls cost 22 tokens a minute. You start with 50 tokens free — no card required.
