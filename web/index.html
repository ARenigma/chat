How it works

Sign in with Google

No phone number, no forms. We read your name and email, and neither is shown to anyone you talk to — you pick a separate display name.

Pick someone who’s free

Everyone shows their age, the languages they speak, how they describe themselves, and how quickly they usually reply.

One message, one token

You start with 50, free. Their replies cost you nothing. If someone never answers, the token comes back.

The rules

  • Everyone who listens is 18 or over. We check the date of birth.
  • You can block anyone instantly. They are not told.
  • Reporting a message sends the message itself to our team.
  • Deleting your account deletes your conversations with it.
  • No contacts, no location, no photos, no advertising ID.
