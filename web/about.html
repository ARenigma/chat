About & contact

Who runs this, what it is, and how to reach a person about it.

What Pappal is

Pappal is a social conversation platform built around respectful one-to-one interaction. Members discover reviewed listener profiles, choose a language, and connect through text, voice, or video.

Pappal does not provide medical advice, counselling, or emergency services.

How it works

For members

Sign in, choose a display name, browse by language and availability, then start a text, voice, or video conversation.

For listeners

Apply from inside the app, write a short introduction, choose the languages you speak, and agree to the community standards. Applications are reviewed before profiles appear.

Safety

Every conversation has block and report controls. Sexual content, harassment, bullying, hate speech, and attempts to move conversations off-platform are prohibited.

Supabase (PostgreSQL) for the database, Google Firebase for notifications, Sentry for crash reports. Crash reports are scrubbed of message contents before they leave the device. The full detail is on the privacy page.
