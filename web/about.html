About & contact

Who runs this, what it is, and how to reach a person about it.

What Pappal is

A paid chat and calling service that connects people who want to talk with people who are willing to listen. Members browse who is available, start a conversation, and pay per minute for the time they spend. The people who listen are ordinary adults who applied and were admitted — not clinicians.

Pappal is peer support and company, not therapy, counselling, or medical care.

How it works

For members

Sign in with Google, browse who is available, and start a chat or call. You begin with 50 free tokens (1 token = ₹1). Chat and audio cost 22 tokens a minute; video costs 35. A message that is never answered refunds its tokens automatically.

For partners (people who listen)

Apply from inside the app. You confirm you are over 18, write a short introduction, and choose the languages you speak. Applications are reviewed by a person. You earn per minute of active conversation.

Safety

Every conversation can be reported from the message itself, and reports go to a human. Blocking is immediate. Only the two people in a conversation can read it — enforced by the database, not by a promise.
