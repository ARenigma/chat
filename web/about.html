Who runs this, what it is, and how to reach a person about it.

What CompanionChat is

A paid chat service that connects people who want to talk with people who are willing to listen. Members start a conversation and spend tokens to send messages; the people who listen are ordinary adults who applied and were admitted, not clinicians.

It is peer support and company, not therapy, counselling or medical care.

How it works

For members

Sign in, see who is free, start talking. 50 tokens to begin and 25 free every day; one token sends one message. A message that is never answered refunds its token automatically.

For people who listen

Apply from inside the app. You confirm you are over 18, write a short introduction, and choose the languages you speak. Applications are reviewed by a person.

Safety

Every conversation can be reported from the message itself, and reports go to a human. Blocking is immediate and mutual. Only the two people in a conversation can read it — enforced by the database, not by a promise.

Contact

testrelay97@gmail.com reaches a person for support, privacy requests, abuse reports and press. We answer in English and Hindi.

Where your data lives

Supabase (PostgreSQL) for the database, Google Firebase for notifications, Sentry for crash reports. Crash reports are scrubbed of message contents before they are sent. The full detail is on the privacy page.
