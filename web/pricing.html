Pricing

You pay for the time you spend. Tokens are the unit — 1 token equals ₹1. You start with 50 tokens free, no card required.

How tokens work

Tokens are the currency inside Pappal. They are not a subscription — you recharge when you want, spend only while you are in a session, and carry your balance forward indefinitely. There is no expiry.

An unanswered text message costs nothing. If you send a message and the other person never replies, the tokens are returned automatically — that is a rule enforced in the database, not a support policy you have to invoke.

Per-minute rates

Type Cost per minute Equivalent in ₹
Text chat 22 tokens / min ₹22 / min

Tokens are deducted continuously while a session is active. You can end a session at any time — you are only charged for the time you actually spend.

Free tokens

Every new account starts with 50 free tokens — enough for roughly 2 minutes of chat. No card required to sign up.

What you are not charged for

  • Browsing available partners
  • Reading replies you receive
  • Messages that go unanswered (tokens returned automatically)
  • Time spent in the app outside an active session
