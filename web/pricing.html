Access and pricing

Pappal is designed to make starting and managing a conversation clear, predictable, and easy to control. Browsing profiles and managing your account are free.

Choose a format

Start with text, switch to voice, or use video when it suits the conversation. Availability depends on the listener and the live service controls shown in the app.

You stay in control

Leave a conversation at any time. Mute, block, and report controls are always available, and presence starts hidden until you choose otherwise.

Optional prepaid access

Conversations use prepaid tokens when paid access is enabled. One token costs ₹1. The current rates are ₹22 per minute for text chat, ₹22 per minute for voice, and ₹35 per minute for video. The app always shows the applicable rate before a conversation starts.

Recharge packs are available for ₹100, ₹300, ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹2,500, and ₹5,000. There are no subscriptions or automatic renewals. The final amount is shown before payment.

What is always available

  • Browsing listener profiles and languages
  • Managing your privacy and presence choices
  • Blocking and reporting
  • Deleting your account and data

Questions about a transaction?

If a paid feature is shown in your version of the app, its terms are displayed before purchase. See the refund policy for support details.
