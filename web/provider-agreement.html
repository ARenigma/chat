Provider agreement

Effective: 10 October 2026

This Provider Agreement ("Agreement") is between you ("Provider", "you") and the operator of the Pappal platform ("Pappal", "we", "us"). By accepting, you confirm you have read and agree to all terms below.

1. Independent Provider, Not an Employee

You provide services on Pappal as an independent, self-employed service provider. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, franchise, joint-venture, or partnership relationship between you and Pappal. You are responsible for your own tools, working hours, and the manner of your services.

2. Earnings and Revenue Share

For each completed paid minute of a conversation on Pappal, you receive the provider share set in the platform configuration (currently 60% of the session value). The platform retains the remainder to cover payment processing, infrastructure, and operations. The share percentage may change; we will give you reasonable notice before any change takes effect.

3. Payouts

Earnings are held for a 7-day settlement period after each session. Payouts are processed weekly for accumulated balances of ₹500 or more, transferred to the bank account or UPI address you registered during onboarding — verified in your name. Balances below ₹500 carry forward to the following week. Payouts may be withheld during an active investigation of a reported violation or a chargeback under review.

4. Taxes

Where required by law, tax deducted at source (TDS) under Section 194-O of the Income Tax Act will be deducted from your earnings before payout, and remitted to the government on your behalf. You are solely responsible for filing your own income tax returns, any applicable GST registration and compliance, and all other tax obligations arising from your earnings on the platform. We will provide payout records on request.

5. Conduct

As a Provider you agree not to:

  • Use sexual, abusive, or threatening language with any member.
  • Share or solicit phone numbers, social media handles, email addresses, or any other means of off-platform contact.
  • Accept or arrange payment outside the Pappal platform.
  • Present yourself as a licensed medical professional, lawyer, financial adviser, or other regulated professional, or offer advice as a replacement for qualified professional guidance. Astrologers' readings are for guidance and entertainment only and must be presented as such.
  • Discuss or facilitate any illegal activity.

6. Member Privacy

Members appear on Pappal under pseudonyms. You must not attempt to identify them outside the platform, contact them through any channel they did not initiate on Pappal, or disclose any information about your conversations to third parties.

7. Suspension and Termination

Pappal may suspend or terminate your access for violation of this Agreement, repeated member complaints, or chargebacks under investigation. Earnings may be withheld during an active investigation. Where practicable, we will notify you of the reason and give you an opportunity to respond before a final decision is made.

8. Changes to this Agreement

We may update this Agreement from time to time. We will notify you through the app before material changes take effect. Continuing to use the platform after a change's effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.

9. Contact and Grievance Redressal

For questions, disputes, or grievance redressal, contact us through the Support section of the app. We aim to respond to grievances within 48 hours.
