Refund & cancellation policy

Clear rules about what can be refunded, how to ask, and when you can expect it. Written for users in India.

Automatic refunds — unanswered messages

If you send a text message and the other person never replies, the tokens you spent on that message are returned to your balance automatically. This happens in the database without you having to do anything — there is no timeout to wait for, no support ticket to open.

Unused token balance

Tokens you have purchased but not yet spent can be refunded on request, subject to the following:

  • The refund is for the rupee value of the unredeemed tokens in the pack you purchased (1 token = ₹1).
  • Any payment gateway processing fee charged to us for the original transaction may be deducted from the refund amount.
  • Free tokens (the 50 you received when you joined) are not refundable — they were never charged.
  • Tokens that have already been spent in sessions are not refundable — you received the service for those minutes.

Cancellation

There is no subscription to cancel. Pappal is pay-as-you-go: you recharge when you want and spend only while in a session. You are never charged automatically, so there is nothing to "cancel" beyond simply not recharging.

If you wish to stop using the service entirely, you can delete your account from inside the app. See delete your data for instructions.

How to request a refund

Email us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} from the address you used to sign in, with the subject line "Refund request". Include:

  • The transaction ID or UPI reference number from your recharge
  • The amount you recharged and the date
  • Your remaining token balance (visible in the app)
  • The reason for the refund request

We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and returned to the original payment method. UPI refunds typically appear within 1–3 business days of processing; card refunds may take up to 5–7 additional business days depending on your bank.

When refunds are not available

  • Tokens already spent in sessions — the conversation happened and the partner's time was given.
  • Requests made more than 90 days after the original recharge.
  • Accounts that were suspended for violating the terms of use. We will not refund a balance on a suspended account in general, but may do so in exceptional circumstances at our discretion.

Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, or if you are not satisfied with how your refund request was handled, you may raise a formal complaint through our grievance process. We respond to all grievance filings within the timelines stated there.
