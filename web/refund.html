Refund & cancellation policy

Clear rules about what can be refunded, how to ask, and when you can expect it. Written for users in India.

When this policy applies

Some versions of Pappal may offer optional paid features. If no paid feature appears in your app, there is no purchase or subscription to cancel.

Eligible purchases

Unused purchased balance may be refundable on request, subject to the purchase method, applicable law, and these conditions:

  • The request relates to a completed purchase shown in your history.
  • The purchased balance has not already been used.
  • The request is made within 90 days of purchase.
  • Promotional or complimentary balance is not refundable.

Cancellation

Pappal does not use automatic subscription renewals. You can stop using the service at any time or delete your account from the app.

If you wish to stop using the service entirely, you can delete your account from inside the app. See delete your data for instructions.

How to request a refund

Email us at hello@pappal.live from the address you used to sign in, with the subject line "Refund request". Include:

  • The transaction ID or purchase reference
  • The purchase amount and date
  • The unused balance shown in the app, if applicable
  • The reason for the refund request

We acknowledge refund requests within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days and returned to the original payment method. Your bank or app store may require additional processing time.

When refunds are not available

  • Balance or features that have already been used.
  • Requests made more than 90 days after purchase.
  • Accounts that were suspended for violating the terms of use. We will not refund a balance on a suspended account in general, but may do so in exceptional circumstances at our discretion.

Disputes

If you believe a charge was made in error, or if you are not satisfied with how your refund request was handled, you may raise a formal complaint by writing to us at hello@pappal.live. We respond within a reasonable time.
