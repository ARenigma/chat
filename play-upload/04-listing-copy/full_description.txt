Positive conversations, made simple.

Pappal is a welcoming social conversation platform. Discover reviewed listener
profiles, choose a language, and connect through text, voice, or video.

HOW IT WORKS

Sign in, choose a display name, and browse profiles by language, interests, and
availability. Start with text, or choose voice or video when it suits the
conversation.

SAFETY BY DEFAULT

Listener applications are reviewed before profiles appear. Clear community
standards prohibit age-inappropriate content, grooming, bullying, harassment,
hate speech, impersonation, and attempts to move conversations off-platform.

Block and report controls are available throughout the app. Blocking is
immediate, and reports are reviewed by a person.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Conversations are protected by database access controls. When a session ends,
its content disappears from both participants' apps immediately. An inaccessible
copy is retained for 30 days for account disputes and safety review, then
permanently deleted.

Pappal does not request your contacts or precise location and does not use an
advertising ID. Photos, camera, and microphone are accessed only when you start
a feature that needs them.

You can manage presence, choose Light, Dark, or System appearance, and delete
your account directly from the You tab.

PLEASE NOTE

Pappal is a social conversation platform. It does not provide medical advice,
counselling, or emergency services. If you need urgent help, contact local
emergency services or a recognised helpline.
